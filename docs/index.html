TN Calendar & Tasks

A Thunderbird extension by TNCode.

TN Calendar & Tasks is a Mozilla Thunderbird extension in development for Google Calendar synchronization, event reminders and operating system notifications. Google Tasks support is planned and is not implemented in the current development version.

Current development scope

  • Shows the calendars of your Google account in Thunderbird's calendar and keeps events in sync.
  • The current development version includes event reminders and system notifications that have not yet been tested in Thunderbird.

Why it asks for access to your Google account

The current development version asks you to sign in with Google and requests Google Calendar and Google Tasks permissions. Calendar access is used for calendar features; the Tasks permission is requested even though Tasks functionality is not yet implemented. Permissions will be reviewed before distribution.
